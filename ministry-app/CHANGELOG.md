# JSCI Ministry - what's new

Each section is the release note shown on sanctuaryhub.vercel.app/ministrymobileapp.
Add a section for a new version before running `npm run release`.

## 1.3.0

- Home has a month calendar: go back to past months or ahead, and see your status on every Sunday - serving, REST, excused, covered by a sub, or waiting for approval
- Dates read like "NOV 15", and "Led by" shows the Song Leader's name in bold gold
- Search any teammate by name to see their ministry roles and their lineup dates
- Lineups have tabs for Song Lead, Backup Singers, Musicians and Dancers
- Songs play inside the app: tap play by a song, and it keeps playing in a mini player while you read the lyrics
- Lyrics for every lineup song, written by the Media team
- File an excuse for any date ahead on a calendar, with a clear note if you already filed one for that date
- Swap Schedule: trade dates with a teammate who has the same part
- Requests now keep a full history of your excuses, subs and swaps
- Check in at church with GPS (within 1.5 km), see who is already there, and your attendance history
- Group chats: Singers, Musicians, Dancers, Media, Ushers, PAW Team and all JSCI Ministry Members - with sent, delivered and seen ticks
- Schedule Plotters can plot, change and remove lineups, and approve excuses, subs and swaps in the app
- Sign-in says when an email is not registered, and "Keep me signed in" is now a choice
- The tab bar waits until your schedule has loaded

## 1.2.0

- A bigger, clearer tab bar: larger icons, and the page you are on in a gold pill
- One calm loading screen in the middle of the phone, instead of several spinners
- A warm welcome when you sign in, and a goodbye when you sign out
- New-look dialogs for every "Are you sure?" question
- Light / Dark mode now lives in Profile > Appearance, and the sign-in screen remembers it
- The app tells you when a new version is out
- A smaller download

## 1.1.0

- The website's look: its colours, Font Awesome icons and the Outfit font
- Light and dark mode
- Android's Home and Back buttons hide while you use the app (swipe up to show them)
- The field you are typing in always stays above the keyboard
- The logo without the black background
